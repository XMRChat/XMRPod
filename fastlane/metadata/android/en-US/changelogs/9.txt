- Replaced podcast-metadata-based XMRChat search with privacy-preserving local matching. XMRPod downloads a generic list of public XMRChat creator pages with a registered podcast RSS link and matches your subscribed feeds on your device. The app no longer sends podcast titles, authors, feed hosts, or episode titles to the server.
- Creator discovery now also matches subscribed podcasts by their declared website URL (full URL, path-aware), in addition to RSS feed URL. Website URLs claimed by more than one creator are skipped rather than guessed.
- Send a source=xmrpod marker when creating an XMRChat tip so the server can tell tips came from XMRPod, without tracking what you listen to.
- Added a first-launch privacy disclosure explaining how XMRChat creator discovery works.
