XMRPod is an open-source Android podcast player with Monero tipping support for podcasts that publish compatible funding links.

The playback screen includes a Tip action. When a podcast episode exposes a supported Monero or XMRChat funding link, XMRPod opens a compatible wallet or payment flow so listeners can send a tip.

For XMRChat pages, XMRPod can create a tip payment address with the listener name, amount, and optional message, then hand the payment URI to a Monero wallet such as Cake Wallet.

Network disclosure:

To discover a matching XMRChat tip page, XMRPod automatically sends HTTPS search requests to nest.xmrchat.com. Up to four requests may use the podcast title, podcast author, podcast feed hostname, and episode title as search terms.

In the current release, this lookup can occur automatically, including during app startup before the playback screen or Tip action is opened. It can also occur for podcasts that do not publish a Monero or XMRChat funding link.
