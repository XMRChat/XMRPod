XMRPod is an open-source Android podcast player with Monero tipping support for creators on XMRChat.

The playback screen includes a Tip action for podcasts whose creators have an XMRChat page. To find these podcasts, XMRPod downloads a generic list of public XMRChat creator pages that have a registered podcast RSS link or website link, then matches your subscribed feeds against that list locally on your device — by RSS feed URL or by the podcast's declared website. No information about which podcasts you listen to or are subscribed to is ever sent to any server.

When you choose to send a tip, XMRPod creates a tip payment address with the listener name, amount, and optional message, then hands the payment URI to a Monero wallet such as Cake Wallet. The tip request includes a marker indicating it came from XMRPod.

Network disclosure:

To discover a matching XMRChat tip page, XMRPod automatically sends HTTPS search requests to nest.xmrchat.com. Up to four requests may use the podcast title, podcast author, podcast feed hostname, and episode title as search terms.

In the current release, this lookup can occur automatically, including during app startup before the playback screen or Tip action is opened. It can also occur for podcasts that do not publish a Monero or XMRChat funding link.
